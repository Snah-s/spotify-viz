Spotify Multidimensional Visualization

RadViz, Star Coordinates, Parallel Coordinates, and PCA for exploring audio-feature patterns across Spotify tracks and genres.

Analytical tasks Track clustering by audio features Acoustic vs energetic contrast Temporal and genre-level trends

RadViz

Each feature is an anchor; tracks settle by weighted feature attraction.

Star Coordinates

Tracks are placed by summing normalized values along weighted vectors.

Parallel Coordinates

Brush any axis to highlight tracks matching multidimensional ranges.

PCA Projection

Python preprocessing reduces ten audio features to two components.

Genre Contrast

Genres positioned by acousticness and energy, sized by popularity.

Year Trend

Aggregated feature changes across the 1921-2020 dataset.

Draft Insights

High danceability and energy tend to pull popular tracks toward similar RadViz and Star Coordinate regions.

Acousticness separates many genres from energetic clusters, especially in the genre contrast plot.

Parallel brushing is useful for isolating high-energy or high-acousticness tracks and checking their behavior across all features.
